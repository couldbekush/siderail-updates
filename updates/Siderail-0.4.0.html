New

  • Automatic updates through Sparkle. Siderail checks the update feed once a day and asks before installing; "Check for updates" under Settings checks now. Updates are signed, and installed copies reject any that aren't.
  • Disabled indicator when brightness can't change. Over a screen Siderail can't control (like an iPad), an indicator drawn like the system's appears with its slider disabled and the sun red and crossed out, shivering with each warning buzz. On macOS 26 it's the Liquid Glass bar at the top centre; on earlier versions, the square bezel at the bottom centre.
  • No-start zones. Slides can't start in the bottom 25% or top 10% of an edge, where a hand resting while typing often brushes the trackpad. A slide started elsewhere still moves through them, and corner taps still work there. Both are adjustable under Settings.
  • More corner actions: Play / pause, Next track, Previous track, Mission Control, Screenshot and Lock screen. Double-tapping the Screenshot corner again closes the screenshot toolbar (a recording in progress is left alone).
  • "Click to slide" hint. In Press and slide, sliding along an edge without clicking shows the same box with the current level faded and "Click to slide", so the mode never feels broken.

Fixed

  • An external monitor that isn't ready to answer when it wakes or is plugged in (seen with an LG monitor) is tried again after 2, 5 and 10 seconds, and again when a brightness slide finds it uncontrollable, instead of staying "can't change" until Siderail restarts.
