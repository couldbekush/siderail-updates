New

  • Software dimming. Screens whose brightness can't be set (an iPad, or a monitor that isn't answering) now dim with a see-through black layer instead of refusing, and any screen can go darker than its own minimum: keep sliding down past 0%. Sliding up clears the layer before the real brightness rises. Clicks pass through the layer, and quitting Siderail removes it. The indicator shows "Dimmed" while the layer is in use. Turn it off with Settings → Extra dimming, which brings back the "can't change" warning.
